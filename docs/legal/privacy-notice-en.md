# Privacy Notice for OptiAssist

**Version 2026-05-18**

This privacy notice describes how personal data about you is processed when you use OptiAssist as an employee, technician, operator, or other user of a company that has entered into an agreement with Optipeople ApS regarding OptiAssist.

This notice has been prepared in accordance with Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Who is the data controller

**Your employer is the data controller** for the personal data processed in OptiAssist about you as an employee. Your employer has decided to use OptiAssist and sets the purposes and parameters of the processing.

**Optipeople ApS is a data processor** and processes your information on behalf of your employer under a separate data processing agreement.

Requests regarding your rights (see section 7) must be directed to your employer. Optipeople will assist your employer in responding to such requests to the extent necessary and required by law.

## 2. What personal data we process

We process the following categories of data about you:

- **Identification and contact data**: name, email address, username, and your role at your employer.
- **Usage data**: the questions you ask OptiAssist and the answers you receive, conversation history, and the selected machine or Knowledgebase.
- **Technical metadata**: timestamps for questions and answers, sources used, document versions, events, and system logs.
- **Session and access data**: user ID, session information, IP address, and browser agent (User-Agent).
- **Attachments**: images or files that you attach to questions in OptiAssist.
- **Language preference**: your selected language setting.
- **Optional analytics consent**, if you have given it.

We do not collect special categories of personal data (for example health data). If you yourself enter such data into a question or attachment, it falls outside the agreed purpose.

## 3. Purposes and legal basis

The processing is carried out in order to:

1. Provide OptiAssist to you and to your employer.
2. Ensure traceability, audit, and reconstruction of historical answers.
3. Provide support, debug issues, and improve the specific setup at your employer.
4. Protect against misuse and maintain the security of the service.
5. Fulfil the agreement between your employer and Optipeople.

**The legal basis** is primarily the data processing agreement between your employer and Optipeople, together with your employer's legal basis for processing data about you as an employee — typically Article 6(1)(b) (contract) or Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests) of the GDPR.

If you have given consent to optional product analytics, that data is processed on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). You may withdraw your consent at any time — this does not affect your ability to use OptiAssist.

## 4. Recipients and sub-processors

To deliver OptiAssist, Optipeople uses a number of technical sub-processors that process personal data on Optipeople's behalf:

- **Anthropic** — large language model (calls are processed outside the EU/EEA, primarily in the United States, on the basis of EU Standard Contractual Clauses or another valid transfer mechanism).
- **Voyage AI** — embedding model (may involve processing outside the EU/EEA on the same basis as above).
- **Supabase** — database component, hosted in Frankfurt (EU).
- **Microsoft Azure** — object storage and hosting, hosted in the EU.
- Additional technical sub-processors as required for operation and delivery.

Optipeople does not use your questions, answers, or other customer data to train general AI models. Where contractually possible, the same is ensured at our AI sub-processors.

The current list of sub-processors is set out in the data processing agreement between your employer and Optipeople.

## 5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Model calls to Anthropic and Voyage AI may result in data being transferred to the United States or other countries outside the EU/EEA. Such transfers take place on the basis of EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or another valid transfer mechanism.

## 6. Retention period

The specific retention period for conversation history, logs, and document versions is set in the agreement between your employer and Optipeople. As a general rule, data is retained for as long as is necessary to:

- Provide the service during the agreed subscription period.
- Ensure traceability and audit of past answers.
- Comply with statutory retention requirements.

When the purpose ends, the data is deleted or anonymised. Deletion of personal data is governed by the data processing agreement between your employer and Optipeople.

## 7. Your rights

You have the following rights under the GDPR:

- The right of access to the personal data processed about you.
- The right to rectification of inaccurate data.
- The right to erasure ("the right to be forgotten").
- The right to restriction of processing.
- The right to object to processing.
- The right to data portability.
- The right to withdraw any consent given.
- The right to lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency ([datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk)).

Because your employer is the data controller, requests concerning these rights should as a rule be directed to your employer. If you contact Optipeople directly, we will forward the request to your employer.

## 8. Security

Optipeople applies reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access control, role-based access, encryption in transit, logging, backup, and procedures for handling security incidents.

## 9. Cookies

OptiAssist only uses technical cookies that are necessary to log in and maintain an active session. We do not use marketing cookies or third-party trackers.

## 10. Changes

Optipeople may update this privacy notice. If material changes are made, you will be asked to review and confirm the new version the next time you log in.

## 11. Contact

Questions about this privacy notice or about the processing of your personal data should as a rule be directed to your employer. Optipeople ApS may be contacted via your employer's contact person or via Optipeople's general contact details.

---

*This privacy notice supplements the data processing agreement entered into between your employer and Optipeople ApS. In the event of any inconsistency, the data processing agreement takes precedence.*
