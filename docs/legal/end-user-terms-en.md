# End-User Terms of Use for OptiAssist

**Version 2026-05-18**

These terms describe how you may use OptiAssist as an employee, technician, operator, or other user of a company that has entered into an agreement with Optipeople ApS regarding OptiAssist.

These terms supplement the master agreement between your employer and Optipeople. The master agreement is the authoritative contract between your employer and Optipeople. These end-user terms are a short, user-facing version of the rules that already apply to your use of the tool.

## 1. What OptiAssist is

OptiAssist is an AI-based assistant that helps you find information in your company's own machine-specific documentation. You can ask questions about a specific machine or process and receive answers based on the manuals, error codes, procedures, and instructions that your company has uploaded to the tool.

**OptiAssist is a decision-support tool. It is not an authoritative source.**

The original manuals, instructions, procedures, safety rules, and statutory documents used by your company are always the authoritative source — regardless of what OptiAssist replies.

## 2. How you must use OptiAssist

When you use OptiAssist, you accept the following ground rules:

1. **Always verify.** AI answers may contain errors, omissions, or imprecise interpretations. Check the answer against the original manuals or procedures before acting on it.
2. **Follow your company's official procedures.** If an answer from OptiAssist conflicts with your company's procedures or safety rules, follow the procedures — not OptiAssist.
3. **Use source references.** When OptiAssist shows sources or document excerpts, open them and read the basis for the answer, especially before performing actions on the machine.
4. **Escalate when in doubt.** If an answer appears uncertain, incomplete, or contradictory, ask a qualified colleague, an authorised technician, or escalate via the tool's built-in function.

## 3. Safety-critical use

**OptiAssist must not be the sole basis for safety-critical decisions or actions.**

This applies, among other things but not exclusively, to:

- Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedures
- Work on electrical installations
- Work on rotating machine parts
- Work on pressurised equipment or hydraulics
- Work in ATEX zones or with chemicals
- Intervention in safety circuits, emergency stops, or guarding
- Bypassing of safety functions
- Changes to PLC programs or process parameters with safety implications
- Any action where a fault may result in personal injury, production damage, or environmental damage

In these situations you must always follow your company's authoritative sources and internal procedures and, where necessary, contact authorised personnel.

## 4. What you must not use OptiAssist for

You must not use OptiAssist to:

1. Circumvent your company's own safety procedures.
2. Make automated safety-critical decisions without human oversight.
3. Replace authorised technicians, original manuals, or statutory instructions.
4. Upload material that you or your company do not have the right to use.
5. Submit special categories of personal data (for example health data) without a specific agreement with your company.
6. Reverse-engineer, copy, or attempt to compromise OptiAssist.
7. Monitor colleagues without a lawful basis provided by your employer.
8. Engage in discriminatory, harassing, or unlawful processing.

You are responsible for all activity that takes place via your login credentials. Contact your administrator or Optipeople immediately if you suspect unauthorised access.

## 5. What we log about your use

OptiAssist logs, among other things, the questions you ask, the answers you receive, the sources used, timestamps, the selected machine, and your user ID. Logging is performed for traceability, support, troubleshooting, quality assurance, and audit.

A short description of which data we process about you, why, and for how long is set out in the [Privacy Notice](./privacy). If you have questions about your rights as a data subject, please contact your employer, who is the data controller for the processing.

## 6. Changes to these terms

Optipeople may update these end-user terms. If material changes are made, you will be asked to review and accept the new version the next time you log in.

## 7. Relationship to the master agreement

These end-user terms are derived from the master agreement between your employer and Optipeople. In the event of any inconsistency, the master agreement and any data processing agreement take precedence.

This agreement is governed by Danish law.
